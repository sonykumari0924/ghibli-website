Studio Ghibli
Studio Ghibli is a world-renowned Japanese animation studio known for its captivating storytelling, stunning hand-drawn animation, and deep emotional resonance. Founded in 1985 by visionary directors Hayao Miyazaki and Isao Takahata, along with producer Toshio Suzuki, the studio has produced some of the most beloved animated films in cinema history.
From the magical adventures in My Neighbor Totoro and Spirited Away to the poignant narratives of Grave of the Fireflies and Princess Mononoke, Studio Ghibli's films transcend cultural boundaries and resonate with audiences of all ages. Each film is a testament to the power of imagination, exploring themes of nature, love, courage, and the human spirit.
Whether you're a longtime fan or new to the world of Ghibli, this page offers a glimpse into the studio's rich legacy through trailers, movie lists, and more. Welcome to the enchanting world of Studio Ghibli, where every story is a journey into the heart.
 
 
Ghibli Previews
 
 

Spirated Away

Spirited Away follows the journey of a ten-year-old girl named Chihiro, who stumbles into a mysterious and magical world after her parents unwittingly transform into pigs. As her parents are trapped in this strange realm, Chihiro must navigate a fantastical and often perilous world of spirits, witches, and mythical creatures to save her family and find her way back home.
In this enchanting film, Chihiro finds work at a bathhouse run by the enigmatic witch Yubaba, where she adopts the name "Sen" and gradually uncovers her inner strength and resilience. Along the way, she encounters a host of memorable characters, including the mysterious and silent Haku, and the gluttonous No-Face, each contributing to her growth and understanding of the magical world she is now part of.
Spirited Away is a visually stunning and emotionally rich film that explores themes of identity, courage, and the clash between the mundane and the magical. With its imaginative world-building, breathtaking animation, and a poignant story, it has captured the hearts of audiences worldwide and remains one of Studio Ghibli's most acclaimed works.
 
 

Boy and the Heron

Boy and the Heron , directed by Hayao Miyazaki, is a captivating animated film from Studio Ghibli that immerses viewers in a blend of reality and fantasy. The story follows Mahito, a young boy struggling with the recent loss of his mother and the difficulties of adjusting to life in the countryside with his father. His world changes dramatically when he encounters a mystical heron that leads him to a hidden, magical realm. This enchanting world is filled with whimsical creatures and secrets that challenge Mahito’s perceptions and push him to confront his inner fears.
As Mahito embarks on his extraordinary journey, he discovers the transformative power of imagination and the importance of facing personal challenges. The film delicately explores themes of grief and healing, offering a heartfelt portrayal of Mahito’s emotional journey and growth. Through stunning hand-drawn animation, Miyazaki brings this fantastical realm to life with intricate detail and vibrant colors, creating a visually captivating experience.
"The Boy and the Heron" continues Studio Ghibli’s tradition of exceptional storytelling and artistic excellence, reinforcing Miyazaki’s reputation as a master of animated cinema. The film’s rich narrative and beautiful animation make it a timeless addition to the studio’s beloved filmography, resonating with audiences of all ages and offering a magical escape into a world of wonder and self-discovery.
 
 
 
 
 

Kiki's Delivery Service

Kiki's Delivery Service is a charming and heartwarming animated film directed by Hayao Miyazaki and produced by Studio Ghibli. The story follows Kiki, a young witch who, as part of a traditional coming-of-age rite, moves to a new city to live independently. Armed with her magical broomstick and a spirit of determination, Kiki starts a delivery service to support herself. Along the way, she encounters various challenges and meets a range of quirky characters who help her navigate the complexities of her new life.
As Kiki adjusts to her new environment, the film explores themes of self-reliance, personal growth, and the struggle to maintain one’s identity in a changing world. The narrative beautifully captures Kiki’s journey of self-discovery and the ups and downs of finding one’s place in the world. With its endearing characters and whimsical animation, "Kiki's Delivery Service" offers a delightful blend of magic and everyday life, celebrating the joy of adventure and the importance of perseverance.
The film’s enchanting visuals and Miyazaki’s signature storytelling style create a captivating experience that appeals to audiences of all ages. "Kiki's Delivery Service" remains a beloved classic in the Studio Ghibli canon, known for its heartwarming message and imaginative world-building.
 
 
 

Ponyo

Ponyo , directed by Hayao Miyazaki, is a charming animated film from Studio Ghibli that tells the enchanting story of a young goldfish princess named Ponyo who dreams of becoming human. When Ponyo escapes from her underwater kingdom and makes contact with a young boy named Sosuke, she transforms into a human girl with magical abilities. The film explores their burgeoning friendship and the consequences of Ponyo's transformation on the natural world.
As Ponyo and Sosuke's bond deepens, the balance between land and sea is disrupted, causing a series of fantastical and visually stunning events. The film's narrative highlights themes of love, friendship, and environmental harmony, emphasizing the impact of human actions on nature. Miyazaki's signature hand-drawn animation brings the oceanic world and Ponyo’s magical adventures to life with vibrant colors and whimsical designs, creating a visually captivating and heartwarming experience.
"Ponyo" is celebrated for its imaginative storytelling and its ability to appeal to audiences of all ages. It reflects Studio Ghibli's dedication to creating beautiful, thought-provoking films that resonate with viewers through their enchanting visuals and heartfelt narratives.
 
 
 
 
 

The Wind Rises

The Wind Rises directed by Hayao Miyazaki, is a masterfully crafted animated film from Studio Ghibli that tells the story of Jiro Horikoshi, a visionary aeronautical engineer whose work shaped Japan's aviation history. The film follows Jiro’s journey from a young boy dreaming of flight to becoming the designer of the Mitsubishi A6M Zero, a plane used in World War II. Set against the backdrop of early 20th-century Japan, the narrative explores Jiro's passion for creating beautiful aircraft, even as he faces the ethical challenges of his creations being used for war.
The film delves into the personal and professional struggles Jiro encounters as he navigates a rapidly changing world. His love for aviation is juxtaposed with the harsh realities of the Great Kanto Earthquake, economic hardship, and the impending war. Alongside his career, Jiro experiences a poignant romance with Nahoko, a young woman whose love provides him with comfort but also deep sorrow as she battles a terminal illness. Their relationship adds an emotional depth to the story, highlighting the intersection of dreams and the fragility of life.
Visually stunning and rich in detail, "The Wind Rises" is a thoughtful meditation on the pursuit of dreams, the moral implications of creation, and the impact of one’s work on the world. Miyazaki’s final directorial work before his temporary retirement, the film captures the beauty of flight and the inevitable winds of change. With its reflective tone and complex themes, "The Wind Rises" is a powerful exploration of ambition, love, and the bittersweet nature of progress.
